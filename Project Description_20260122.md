**PROJECT DESCRIPTION**  
**Data warehousing and decision support systems (CO4031)**  
By: Assoc. Prof. Dr. Võ Thị Ngọc Châu

**1\. Objectives**

The objectives of this project are listed as follows:

- To enable the students to achieve the course learning outcomes by reviewing and applying the related knowledge and skills in a practical application domain with one specific topic of the course  
- To encourage the students to take advantage of modern technologies for decision making support  
- To enhance the teamwork skills of the students by randomly forming groups

**2\. Course learning outcomes**

Everything you can complete with this project is related to all the knowledge and skills expected from the subject. They are the course learning outcomes of the subject.

**3\. Project details**

**3.1. Grouping**

You are asked to work in group and a random group is formed for each of you. Please set up your working environment, group objectives, group agreement, and assign a role to each member. If you find it difficult to work in such a random group, I will consider your own group but that is not expected. All group issues will be resolved in the first three weeks. After that, it’s supposed that you are OK with your current groups.

**3.2. Decision support solution**

You are asked to define a particular business problem related to decision making support in a selected application domain. After that, define a solution to your chosen business problem. In your solution, you are asked to use the given specific topic of the course. 

A business problem needs to be defined at least with the following aspects:

- Context in an organization  
- Decision makers  
- Expected goals

In addition, you are asked to develop an application to put your solution in use. Any kind of applications is accepted if you prove its appropriateness in the defined context.

**3.3. Submission**

A due date/time is fixed for every group. All of you will submit the following materials:

i). **A midterm presentation**: \*.pdf. You are asked to prepare for your short midterm presentation about your project with the following details:  
1\. Introduction  
2\. Business problem definition  
3\. Introduction to your proposed solution where ***you present the given presentation topic in detail***  
4\. Plan to complete your project  
5\. Any additional details that you would like to make your presentation interesting about the project

ii). **A final report**: \*.pdf. In the report, you are asked to report your teamwork activities and contribution of each member to the project. The report must be structured as follows:  
	1\. Introduction  
	2\. Business problem definition  
	3\. Proposed solution  
	4\. Application development  
	5\. Evaluation  
	6\. Conclusion  
	References  
	Appendix 1\. Teamwork report  
Other parts can be added as appendices of the report.

iii). **A final presentation**: \*.pdf. You are asked to use the structure described above for your presentation.

iv). **A demonstration video**: \*.mp4. You can prepare a video in any way you want to demonstrate your application. The length of the video is at most 30 minutes.

All the submitted files are required in the specified format and named with your group number.

**4\. In-class presentation**

Each group is given **10 minutes for a short midterm presentation** and **20 minutes for a final presentation and demonstration** in class. You are expected to present your project and demonstrate your application. Each member is expected to make an equal contribution to the presentations and present your part in the given periods of time in an interactive manner.
